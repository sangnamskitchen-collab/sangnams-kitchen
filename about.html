About Us

Hi, we're Sangnam's Kitchen!

Homemade Korean favorites, cooked fresh for our neighbors in Acacia Estates, Taguig.

Our Story

It all started on the pickleball court. Our morning Ivory Wood pickleball group, Morning Puksaan Group, fell in love with my mom's Korean food when she shared it with them after a game.

Since May 25, 2026, Sangnam's Kitchen has been cooking for more neighbors every week.

Sangnam is our dog. Around Acacia Estates, people know him better than they know our name, so he became our mascot.

Meet Mom

Meet Lity, the Korean mom behind every dish. She loves to cook, and she loves sharing authentic Korean food and our culture with the people around her.

Real Korean Flavor

About 90% of our ingredients come from Korean suppliers.

Mom's rule is simple: always serve our customers the best quality we can.

Our Cooking Days

We cook fresh for you on a set schedule:

  • Wednesday: delivered before dinner
  • Friday: delivered before lunch or dinner
  • Sunday: delivered before lunch

Delivery and Payment

Delivery is free within Acacia Estates. We try our best to make sure every order reaches you fresh and on time.

We accept bank transfer, InstaPay, GCash, and cash.
