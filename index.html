How to Order
  1. 1
    Choose your dishes. Tap + under the size you want. Tap − to lower the number.
  2. 2
    Open your cart. Tap Cart at the top to review your order and total.
  3. 3
    Send your order. Tap Send My Order and choose Viber, Messenger, or any app. Then tap Send in that chat.

Did not see the app list? Tap Copy Order, open Viber, Messenger, or Instagram, press and hold the message box, and tap Paste.

Viber, Messenger, and Instagram must be installed on your phone. Open this page in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android), not inside the Facebook or Instagram app.
